- Setup wizard hints checked step by step on a Samsung (One UI 9): step 1 shows 4 Android windows and location/microphone have no "Allow" — the hint now says "While using the app"; step 5 on Samsung is "OK".
- Accessibility service name in Ukrainian and Russian ("XiaoDroidLink: керування з машини" / "управление с машины"); it was English there.
- Phone app buttons: long translations shrink to fit instead of being cut (Russian "Что-то не работает? Отправить отчёт").
- Send report: opens the mail apps themselves — one directly, several in a chooser of mail apps only — and reminds to tap Send. The mailto selector failed with Gmail + Outlook and the app said "no mail app".
- Guide (uk, en, fr, es, zh, ru): first setup step by step with the exact Android screens, switches and confirmations, screenshots of the steps and of the report form; Russian guide extended.
