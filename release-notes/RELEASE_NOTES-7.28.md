# XiaoDroidLink 7.28 (versionCode 74)

## What changed

- **Phone language by default.** Until a language is picked in the app, it
  uses the phone's language when it is one of English, Ukrainian, Chinese,
  French or Spanish, and English otherwise. A language picked in the app is
  kept. Users who never picked a language will see their phone's language
  instead of English after the update.
