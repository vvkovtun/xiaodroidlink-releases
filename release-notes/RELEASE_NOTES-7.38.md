# XiaoDroidLink 7.38 (versionCode 84)

## What changed

- After you choose the car in Bluetooth settings, the display goes back to the car by itself. You no longer need to tap "Return to car".
- Texts no longer name one car model, because the app works with any car that supports CarLink.
- New "Contact the developer" button that opens an email with the app version and phone model.
- New Google Play description and screenshots.
