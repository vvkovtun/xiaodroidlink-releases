# XiaoDroidLink 8.89 (versionCode 235) — internal

## What changed

«Модерн» (Modern) widgets now follow Kasım's mockup, not only the cards and colours (Vadym, 21.11: weather and player buttons were not like the mockup). Other designs are unchanged.

- **Weather:** white outline condition icon as tall as the temperature, regular-weight temperature centred, condition · city below, a divider, and «Today / Tomorrow» with a drop and the chance, a yellow sun and a white moon with the day and night temperatures. The drawing canvas follows the card's proportions, so the content fills the card.
- **Clock:** light digits, larger date.
- **Player:** a small pill with the player and ▾, round chips with outline icons (queue, microphone, thumbs down/up), slate prev/next circles, deep indigo play, thin progress with a small white thumb.
- **Other widgets:** flat tiles without gradient and border; outline icons instead of emoji — finish flag (trip, arrival, recent places), bolt (phone charging, request buttons), handset (calls button), home and office (quick actions); call badge on quick-action contacts.
- Catalog previews of Modern refreshed.
