# XiaoDroidLink 7.33 (versionCode 79)

## What changed

- All eight dashboard designs, day and night, now use clear glass widgets over the design's photo instead of solid themed cards. Each design keeps its own button colors and small details.
- New weather widget: a large icon for the current conditions (sun or moon, clouds, fog, rain, heavy rain, snow, thunderstorm), plus today and tomorrow with day and night temperatures and the chance of precipitation. "Rain in 1 h" warns before rain.
- Weather now requests the daily conditions, the precipitation chance and whether it is day or night from Open-Meteo.
