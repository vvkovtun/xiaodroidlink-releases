# XiaoDroidLink 7.40 (versionCode 86)

## What changed

- The phone icon next to the clock on the car screen is now a button: it opens a "Choose your car" window with the phone's live Bluetooth screen, so you can pick the car without taking the phone.
- Tap "✕ Close" or anywhere outside the window to go back to the map. Once calls connect through the car, the window closes by itself.
- If the phone is locked, the window asks you to unlock it first.
- On the phone, a "Calls through the car" button sits right under "Connect" and shows whether calls already go through the car.
- The "Calls through the car are connected" message on the car screen stays until music really plays again, then shows "Music is back in the car".
